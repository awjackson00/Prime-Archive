Privacy and local storage

The tracker does not require accounts, credentials, payment details, Warframe API keys, or personal profiles. The application does not include advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or a user-data backend. Your collection, favorites and preferences are stored locally in this browser under primeArchive.v2. Older versions of local collection storage may be read to migrate your progress. Local storage is used to provide the saved collection feature you request; it remains until you reset or clear site data. Export and import operate locally; your backup is not uploaded to the project or external APIs. Keep exported files on a trusted device.

GitHub Pages hosts this site and logs IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages information and GitHub's privacy statement. Online mode loads equipment and relic images from Warframe Community Developers (cdn.warframestat.us) Warframe Wiki (wiki.warframe.com), and WFCD's public artwork repository via raw.githubusercontent.com, and fetches public relic drop tables from drops.warframestat.us or api.warframestat.us. These services receive ordinary network information such as IP addresses, request timestamps and resource URLs. We do not send your checklist or backup data to them. Their own policies govern their processing and retention; infrastructure may operate outside your country. External links open separate services.

Use local-only mode to prevent the application's external artwork and relic data requests; all nine locally hosted category portraits and collection tracking remain usable. This does not stop hosting-related logging or requests to sites you choose to visit. You can export your progress, reset the collection, or delete all site storage through your browser's settings. A reset clears ownership, component counts, favorites and Founder preference; clearing browser site data also removes the saved empty state. There is no operator-held checklist database to access, correct or delete.

The site is designed for a general gaming audience and does not knowingly request personal information from children. Do not submit personal details in public GitHub issues. If you have privacy, access, deletion or other rights-related questions, contact the maintainer through the repository below using a private channel where available. Applicable statutory rights remain unaffected. For data handled by hosting or external providers, contact that provider. If applicable, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Accessibility

The project aims to make the tracker usable with a keyboard, readable labels, responsive layout, visible focus, and reduced-motion preferences. Relic details are available through hover and a focusable button; text identifies relic era and reward rarity independently of icon colors. If you encounter a barrier, report it through the maintainer's repository. Full WCAG conformity has not been independently audited.
